# MINDPRINT Dynamic Intelligence Model

## Central hypothesis

Static problem-solving ability is only one component of modern cognitive
performance. Intelligence in dynamic information environments may additionally
depend on attention allocation, rapid model formation, adaptive updating,
metacognitive calibration, epistemic judgment, transfer, and appropriate use of
cognitive tools.

The purpose of MINDPRINT is to test this broader hypothesis experimentally.

This hypothesis is not validated by the existence of the simulation platform.
The new constructs are research targets.

## Paradigm shift

The long-term assessment model moves from:

Question → answer → correct / incorrect

toward:

Dynamic environment → observe → allocate attention → form a model → decide →
state confidence → encounter change/evidence → update or resist → transfer.

A micro-environment can therefore produce several observations rather than one
item response.

## Research ontology

### Model Formation

Hypothesized processes include fluid reasoning, structural abstraction, causal
inference, quantitative structure detection, hypothesis formation, and rapid
mental-model construction.

### Attention & Cognitive Control

Hypothesized processes include selective and sustained attention, distraction
resistance, inhibition, attentional switching, interruption recovery, and
priority maintenance.

Interaction-based attention traces are proxies. They are not gaze measurements
and must not be described as eye tracking.

### Learning Dynamics

Hypothesized processes include rule induction, learning rate, feedback
sensitivity, revision, unlearning, interference resistance, change detection,
exploration/exploitation, retention, and transfer.

This is not a visual/auditory learning-style classification.

### Metacognitive Regulation

Hypothesized processes include confidence calibration, error awareness,
uncertainty recognition, information-need recognition, confidence updating,
over/under-confidence, and decision threshold regulation.

### Epistemic Judgment

Hypothesized processes include source credibility, evidence hierarchy,
independence, contradiction handling, misinformation resistance,
correlation/causation, base-rate integration, and belief revision.

### Knowledge Transfer

Hypothesized processes include principle abstraction, near/far transfer,
structural analogy, changed-surface transfer, and transfer under constraints.

### Augmented Cognition

This remains a research construct. Potential processes include appropriate AI
reliance, automation bias, under-reliance, verification, cognitive offloading,
tool selection, confidence transfer, detection of AI overconfidence, and
appropriate override of external assistance.

It must not be presented as a validated intelligence dimension.

## Relationship to the existing six production dimensions

The production dimensions remain unchanged:

- Reasoning
- Adaptive Learning
- Evidence Evaluation
- Information Filtering
- Metacognitive Calibration
- Knowledge Transfer

The dynamic ontology does not replace those production labels in Milestone 14.
It provides a broader research layer that can later be tested against them.

Approximate hypotheses include:

- Reasoning → Model Formation
- Adaptive Learning → Learning Dynamics
- Evidence Evaluation → Epistemic Judgment
- Information Filtering → Attention & Cognitive Control plus Epistemic Judgment
- Metacognitive Calibration → Metacognitive Regulation
- Knowledge Transfer → Knowledge Transfer

Attention & Cognitive Control and Augmented Cognition are new research areas and
are not production score dimensions.

## Construct status

All new constructs are currently hypothesized. Prototype environments are
experimental implementations of those hypotheses. No construct is marked
supported or validated.

## General Dynamic Cognitive Ability

A possible future latent construct called General Dynamic Cognitive Ability can
be investigated, but Milestone 14 does not calculate it.

Future analyses may compare:

- one general factor;
- multiple correlated dynamic-cognition factors;
- a higher-order factor;
- bifactor structures.

The data should determine whether a general factor is defensible.

## IQ guardrail

MINDPRINT does not produce MINDPRINT IQ, Dynamic IQ, Modern IQ, IQ 2.0, or an
equivalent quotient.

An IQ-style standardized quotient would require substantially more evidence,
including reliability, dimensional structure, external and incremental
validity, test-retest evidence, fairness, norms, and standardization.
